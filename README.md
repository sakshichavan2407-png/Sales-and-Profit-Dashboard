# Sales and Profit Dashboard (Excel)

An interactive Excel dashboard that analyses sales and profit across product categories, sub-categories, states, customers and time, built with pivot tables, charts and slicers.

## Project Overview
The goal of this project is to identify what drives revenue and profitability, and where the business loses money. The dashboard lets users explore performance by year, category, sub-category and state.


## Key Metrics (KPIs)
- **Total Sales:** ~$1.93M
- **Total Profit:** ~$248K
- **Profit Margin:** ~12.9%
- **Total Quantity Sold:** 31,508 units

## Dashboard Visuals
- Total sales and total profit KPI cards
- Profit by year, split by category
- Sales by category and sub-category
- Sales by state
- Customer count by year
- Top 5 customers by profit
- Interactive slicers for filtering

## Key Insights
- **Technology** leads in sales (~$704K) and profit (~$122K), while **Furniture** earns only a 2.9% margin despite ~$622K in sales.
- **Tables** (-$12K) and **Bookcases** (-$2K) are loss-making sub-categories, and Supplies is roughly break-even.
- **Copiers** (36.7%) and **Accessories** (24.9%) have the strongest margins among high-value products.
- **California** and **New York** are the top states by sales. New York is highly profitable (23% margin), while **Texas, Ohio and Pennsylvania** lose money despite high sales.
- **2023** was the best year (~$609K sales, ~$82K profit). 2024 has the best margin (15.1%) but covers only part of the year.
- Sales peak in the **November to December** holiday period.
